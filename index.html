Company Introduction

A project site that runs out of diesel loses working hours

Solid Diesel Trading L.L.C. is a Sharjah-based fuel supplier. We supply construction-grade diesel to project sites for heavy equipment, machinery, generators and other approved construction equipment.

We coordinate sourcing, delivery and documentation through our supply and logistics partners, so you deal with one team from the first requirement to the final invoice. We plan supply against each site's daily requirement and document every delivery.
